# Uinta lakes reachable by 4x4 / jeep trail / old logging road

Compiled 2026-09-22. One agent per drainage read every lake's DWR pamphlet notes
(current + previous edition), junesucker.com notes, Cordell Andersen's book notes,
and the drainage/trailhead access text, looking for rough-road access (jeep
trail, 4WD, high clearance, OHV/ATV, logging road, "road ends near"). One more
agent read the full Falcon guide. Quotes are verbatim from those sources. The
pressure and fish-size cross-check at the bottom uses `uinta_lakes.db`:
`fishing_pressure`, stocking history, DWR's 2025 gillnet samples
(`dwr_gillnet_samples`, Whiterocks only), and the Falcon guide's usage rating.

Caveats: the DWR write-ups for most drainages date from 1981–1999 and several
"jeep roads" have since been gated (China G-21, Shingle Creek West P-60,
Broadhead D-40, R.C. No. 1 WR-2's last 1.3 mi). Roads in the Blanchett Park /
Paradise Park area and the Murdock Basin area are the ones every source still
calls active 4WD routes. Verify current MVUM status before committing to a drive.

Legend for the access tag:
- `drive-to` — vehicle reaches the shore or within a couple hundred yards
- `4WD-spur` — a rough spur road puts you within ~1.5 mi
- `jeep-trail-hike` — the road is the rough part; a real hike still follows
- `gated` — described as a road but now closed to vehicles (walkable)

Jed status: 🎣 = CAUGHT, 👥 = OTHERS, blank = not yet.

## Targets by drainage

### Whiterocks (East Fork) — the richest cluster
The Paradise Park → Cliff Lake OHV road (FR 140 / Johnson Creek) is the single
best 4x4 payoff in the range: it puts a truck at a 68-acre lake that is
otherwise an 11-mile round trip, with five more lakes within 1.5 mi of the road end.

### Trailheads the Falcon guide flags as rough (shorten nothing, but need the truck)
- **Hells Canyon Road → Center Park trailhead** (Yellowstone): "a road from hell… steep and very rocky. A high-clearance vehicle is recommended, and the road may require four-wheel drive when wet." Gateway to Swasey Hole (X-58, X-57, X-59, X-60) and Garfield Basin.
- **Grandview trailhead** (Rock Creek): "5 steep, rocky miles… slow going for low-clearance vehicles."
- **East Fork Bear River trailhead**: "You may need a 4WD vehicle for the last mile when wet… hikers can access Boundary Creek from FR 120 or from Wolverine ATV trail 301."
- **West Fork Whiterocks trailhead**: "numerous 4WD roads in the area, so stay on the road most traveled."
- **West Fork Beaver / Middle Beaver trailheads**: "High clearance may be necessary near the end of the route, especially if it has been raining."

## Cross-check: least pressure and/or bigger fish

Method: rank by `fishing_pressure` (Low/Light first), then by hard fish-size
evidence (DWR 2025 gillnet mean/max length, which exists only for Whiterocks),
then by depth (>20 ft = overwinter safety), then by how much the truck actually
saves. Lakes Jed has already caught are excluded from the top tier.

### Tier 1 — go here
| Lake | Why | Truck payoff |
|---|---|---|

### Big-fish angles without low pressure
- **D-33 Iron Mine**: 10–12 in rainbows and tigers stocked every year 2021–2026; frequent winterkill means whatever survives is fresh catchables, not holdovers.
- **WR-64 Chepeta**: gillnet brookies to 16.0, cutts to 16.5; heavy weekend pressure. Ordinary car access.
- **Z-16 Echo**: 44 ft, "large population" of brookies, golden remnants possible but unlikely (last stocked 2014).

### Skip despite road access
Fishless or dead: DF-2, DF-15, GR-63, G-32, G-35, G-102, BR-41, WR-46, RC-43,
probably G-100 and WR-30. Private: BR-8. Heavy pressure with nothing special:
Z-10, Z-11, Z-17, A-1/3/5/6/9, GR-38, GR-40, DG-28, WR-13, WR-18.

### Suggested trip shape
One long weekend out of Paradise Park covers Tier 1's core: drive the Cliff Lake
OHV road (3 hard miles after 6 graded), camp at Cliff, fish Watkins the first
evening, day-trip Workman → Ted's → Wooley → Sand on the old OHV trail, then on
the way out take FR 235 to Angel and the Blanchett Park jeep road for the two
uncaught Kibahs (DF-11, DF-12) and, if legs allow, DF-4. That single trip
touches 8–10 uncaught waters, four of which have the best measured fish in the
range.
