## Location

Chepeta Lake also known as WR-64 is located in Duchesne County, Utah.The lake lies withing the Whiterocks River Drainage of the Uinta Mountains. From Roosevelt take US 40 until your reach Eagle View Elementary School. Turn east directly after the school onto White Rocks Highway which is also called 5750 East. The road eventually turns into Farm Creek Road. After about 20 miles on the road from highway 40 there will be a turn for FR117 also known as Elkhorn Loop. A lot of people will miss the turn but its easy to tell because the road will look like you're entering private property if you miss the turn. You will follow FR117/Elkhorn loop about 14 Miles east until you hit a fork with a sign for Chepeta Lake/Pole Creek Lake. Turn right and follow the road another ten miles. The parking is about 200 yards away from the lake. There is also various spots to unload horse trailers just before the lake.

## Fish Species

Brook Trout, and Rainbow Trout

## Other Info

Chepeta Lake was named after the native american princess chipeta.

## History

From an article in the Salt Lake Tribune, August 29th, 1954. Bill Hurst - Supervisor of the Ashley National Forest, and Dr D. I. Rasmussen - were fishing Chepeta with Tribune Sports writer Don Brooks. They caught black spotted cutthroat, eastern brook trout, and California golden trout. The golden trout were identified by M. J. Madsen, Utah fisheries chief who said goldens were planted in this drainage many years ago. Don caught a 4 1/4 pound eastern brook that was 19 1/2 inches long. Then a 3 1/2 pound brook that was 18 1/2 inches long.
